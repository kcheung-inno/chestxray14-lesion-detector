How to read this honestly.
  • The box-annotated subset of NIH ChestX-ray14 is small: 880 images, 984 boxes. For Nodule it is only 79 boxes and for Mass 85 — split by patient that leaves 125 in training and 39 in validation. Per-class numbers for those two findings are therefore high-variance. Treat the numbers as a proof of concept, not a benchmark.
  • Training used all 8 findings together (~6× more box supervision than a nodule-only run would have) with negative images included so the model can learn to reject.
  • “Matches a gold box” is a loose test — a detection whose centre falls inside a radiologist-drawn box. It is not IoU, and detections are not deduplicated (RF-DETR is NMS-free, so several boxes often fire on one lesion).
  • Single run, single seed. No confidence intervals.
  • Not a medical device. Must not be used for diagnosis or screening. Research use only.
